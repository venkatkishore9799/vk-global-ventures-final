FOUNDER & DIRECTOR

Leadership With Purpose

Venkat Kishore Chikkala

Founder & Director, VK Global Ventures

Venkat Kishore Chikkala is an entrepreneur and business leader with an international portfolio spanning Education, Hospitality, Travel & Tourism, International Recruitment, Construction and Interiors across India and Southeast Asia.

His entrepreneurial vision is focused on creating businesses that connect people, skills, experiences, opportunities and international markets. He has built a presence in the education sector across India and Thailand, with a focus on professional and industry-oriented education and training.

His hospitality interests include restaurant businesses in Malaysia and Thailand. His business portfolio also extends into construction and interior solutions, supporting residential, commercial and business projects.

A key area of his work is creating international career and placement opportunities for hospitality and hotel-management professionals. Through established relationships and tie-ups with hospitality and hotel groups across different markets, he works to connect eligible candidates with opportunities in Singapore, Malaysia, Thailand and selected European countries.

Through Travel & Tourism, he is also focused on creating travel and tourism opportunities that connect people with destinations, hospitality and international experiences.

As Founder & Director, his focus is on building sustainable businesses, developing trusted international partnerships and creating meaningful opportunities that extend beyond geographical boundaries.
